    Limitations

    What is not solved, stated plainly.

    The dataset is 100% SpanishAll 7,454 transcripts are in Spanish. The Portuguese cases were generated by the team, and the customers are from Mexico, Colombia and Argentina.
    Risk is a business rule, not a bank labelThe dataset's outcome columns (SLA, escalation, priority) cannot be learned from the variables (AUC ≈ 0.50). We defined risk with a documented rule and the model learns to reproduce and generalize it.
    The intent corpus is synthetic1,273 phrases from 216 templates, evaluated on templates the model never saw. Real messages are messier: it must be revalidated with real conversations before production.
    The evaluation customer is simulatedThe conversations use real customers and charges, but the "customer" answers with scripted phrases. It measures whether the rules and the flow are correct, not how natural the conversation feels.
    Data with gaps57% of transactions have no USD amount (all USD and some COP/ARS): we fill it with the observed exchange rate (COP 4,000 and ARS 350). 68% of complaints have no claimed amount.
    "Today" is June 18, 2026It is the last date in the transaction sample. "Last 30 days" queries are computed from there.
